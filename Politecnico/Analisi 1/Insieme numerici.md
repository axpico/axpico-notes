## Numeri naturali 
$$\mathbb{N} := \{0,1,2,3,4,5,\dots\} $$
## Numeri interi relativi 
$$\mathbb{Z} := \{\dots,-2,-1,0,1,2,\dots\}$$
## Numeri razionali
$$\mathbb{Q} := \left\{\frac{m}{n} : m,n \in \mathbb{Z},\ n \neq 0\right\}$$


$$\mathbb{N} \subset \mathbb{Z} \subset \mathbb{Q}$$
