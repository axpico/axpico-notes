## Definition

The term "Internet of Things" was coined around **1999** but only became a concrete reality more recently.

**Core idea:** smart, networked computer chips are incorporated into an increasing number of manufactured objects and places — from electrical systems and domestic appliances to buildings, streets, rivers and even items of clothing.

---

## How it works

- Every object can be uniquely identified via an **IP address**
- Objects contain **sensors** that collect data
- Data is shared over an **Internet-like structure**

---

## Connected Devices vs IoT — What's the difference?

|Connected Devices|IoT Devices (narrow definition)|
|---|---|
|Smartphones, PCs, tablets|Industrial, medical, transport sensors|
|~16 billion in use|~10 billion devices|
|Communicate between each other|Communicate **without** human interaction|

> **IoT in the narrow sense** = all kinds of sensors **other than** phones, PCs and tablets that communicate.

---

## Key Numbers

- **16 billion** mobile phones and PCs connected
- **~10 billion** IoT devices (narrow definition)
- **>4 billion** industrial and commercial sensors
- **~1 billion** medical and transport sensors

---

## Brontobytes

A future unit of measurement for the enormous amounts of data generated by IoT sensors.  
The name is a reminder of the **brontosaurus** (the largest dinosaur).  
It represents data volumes too large to fully imagine today.

## Smart City Applications

### Environment

|Application|What it does|
|---|---|
|**Air pollution**|Controls CO₂ from factories, cars, toxic gases from farms|
|**Forest fire detection**|Monitors combustible gases, defines alert zones|
|**Water quality**|Studies suitability for fauna and drinking water|
|**Water leakages**|Detects liquid presence outside tanks, pressure variations|
|**Radiation level**|Distributed measurement near nuclear stations → leakage alerts|

### Infrastructure

|Application|What it does|
|---|---|
|**Smart Roads**|Warning messages and diversions based on weather and traffic|
|**Smart Parking**|Monitors parking space availability in real time|
|**Traffic congestion**|Monitors vehicle and pedestrian flow, optimises routes|
|**Shipment conditions**|Tracks vibrations, container openings, cold chain|
|**Item location**|Finds individual items in warehouses or harbours|

### Digital & Retail

|Application|What it does|
|---|---|
|**Smartphone detection**|Detects iPhones, Androids, and WiFi/Bluetooth devices|
|**Electromagnetic level**|Measures energy from cell stations and WiFi routers|
|**Intelligent shopping**|Advice at point of sale based on habits, allergies, expiry dates|

|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**IoT**|Internet of Things|
|**Sensor**|Device that measures physical data|

|**Brontobyte**|Enormous future data measurement unit|
