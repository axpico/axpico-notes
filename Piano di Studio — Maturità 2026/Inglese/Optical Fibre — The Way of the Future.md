## Current State

- **Copper wire** cables = still the most common mode of Internet transmission
- **Optical fibre** = rapidly growing around the world
- Growth driven by: technological advances + market demand (e.g. China)
- **Fibre optic cabling is the future** of online communication — no question

---

## What is Optical Fibre?

A **network cable** that contains **strands of glass fibres** inside an insulated casing. Transmits data as **pulses of light** (not electrical signals like copper).

---

## Two Primary Types

|Type|Glass strand|Light source|Best for|
|---|---|---|---|
|**Single mode fibre**|Very thin|Laser|Long distance, high precision|
|**Multi-mode fibre**|Thicker|LEDs (light-emitting diodes)|Shorter distances, multimedia|

---

## Advantages Over Copper Cabling

### Speed

- Fibre: up to **10 Gbps**
- Copper: between **10 Mbps and 1 Gbps**

### Distance

- Light can travel **much longer distances** down a fibre cable without losing strength
- → Lessens the need for **signal boosters** (amplifiers to re-strengthen the signal)

### Interference

- Fibre is **less susceptible to interference**
- Can withstand **shock and vibration**
- Withstands **inclement weather** (bad weather conditions)

### Electromagnetic shielding

- Traditional copper cable requires **special shielding** to protect from electromagnetic interference
- Glass/fibre physical properties **naturally avoid** most of these issues

---

## Fibre vs Copper — Quick Comparison

|Feature|Optical fibre|Copper wire|
|---|---|---|
|Speed|Up to 10 Gbps|10 Mbps – 1 Gbps|
|Signal boosters needed|Fewer|Frequent|
|EM interference|Naturally resistant|Needs shielding|
|Weather/vibration|Resistant|Vulnerable|
|Cost|Higher|Lower|

---

## Where Fibre is Used

### Long-distance (main use)

Installed to support **connections between cities and countries**.

### "Last mile" installations

Many Internet providers have invested in extending fibre installations to **suburban neighbourhoods** for **direct access by households**. These are called **"last mile"** installations — the final link from the ISP's network to homes and businesses.

---

## Key Vocabulary

|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|**Optical fibre**|Network cable using glass strands to transmit light signals|
|**Single mode fibre**|Thin glass + laser; long-range (fibra ottica monomodale)|
|**Multi-mode fibre**|Thicker glass + LEDs; shorter range (fibra ottica multimediale)|
|**Signal booster**|Amplifier that restrengthen a weakening signal (amplificatore del segnale)|
|**LED**|Light-emitting diode — produces light used in multi-mode fibre|
|**Inclement weather**|Severe or bad weather conditions|
|**Electromagnetic interference**|Disruption caused by EM fields (e.g. from other cables)|
|**Last mile**|Final section of the network connecting ISP to end users|
|**Insulated casing**|Protective outer layer around cable|
