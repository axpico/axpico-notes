# 📋 Templates

Reusable templates for university. Duplicate any of these when you need a fresh copy.

> 💡 **How to use:** Open a template, click the **•••** menu → **Duplicate**, then rename it and fill it in.
> 

---

## Available Templates

- 🗒️ **Lecture Notes** — One per lecture, structured with key concepts, definitions & questions
- 📄 **Essay / Report** — Outline, thesis, argument blocks, bibliography
- 📚 **Book / Paper Summary** — Structured reading notes
- 📝 **Assignment Brief** — Track one assignment from start to submission
- 🧪 **Lab Report** — Hypothesis, method, results, discussion
- 🎯 **Exam Study Guide** — Topic breakdown, flashcard prompts, past paper notes
- 👥 **Study Group Notes** — Shared session notes with action items
